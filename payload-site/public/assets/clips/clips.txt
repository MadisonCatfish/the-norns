[keys]  keys.webm / keys-poster.jpg
Title: Three Keys on the Hull
Feature: Minimize and compact / full mode keys
Description: Two new keys sit left of Close, as large: minimize glows yellow and hides the body to the tray, the mode key glows green and switches compact and full mode in one click.

[displaypick]  displaypick.webm / displaypick-poster.jpg
Title: Pick Your Power Display
Feature: Power display animations with live previews
Description: Settings → Display shows every animation for the small power display as a live preview: the Working sphere, Cube wave, ノルン and Flow field. Click one and the hull's display switches at once.

[readlogs]  readlogs.webm / readlogs-poster.jpg
Title: Your Logs, Remembered
Feature: Read logs into session statistics
Description: One key on the Statistics page reads your combat logs (read-only) and adds the faction commanders, titans and pirate dreadnoughts found there to the sessions you already have, each spawn counted once.

[oneclick]  oneclick.webm / oneclick-poster.jpg
Title: Update in One Click
Feature: One-click update
Description: When a new release is out, About offers "Update to …". Press it and The Norns downloads the installer from GitHub, checks it against the published SHA-256 and starts it for you.

[rewarp]  rewarp.webm / rewarp-poster.jpg
Title: A Timer That Knows a Re-Warp
Feature: Anomaly timer, re-warp aware
Description: Warp out and the clear time counts up. Warp again without a shot fired and the timer flashes red, rushes a random time down to zero and waits for the next site; after a long idle break it simply stays dark.

# 1.6.0 (12.11.2026): eyecomfort, switchname, alerts

[eyecomfort]  eyecomfort.webm / eyecomfort-poster.jpg
Title: Easy on the Eyes
Feature: Eye comfort and hull colour sliders
Description: Settings → Display → Colours: drag Hull brightness from near-black graphite to light steel and Hull saturation from grey to full colour, 0 being the familiar steel. Then turn on Eye comfort: the accent and lamps go muted and the glow goes, so the game stays in front.

[switchname]  switchname.webm / switchname-poster.jpg
Title: Who's in Front
Feature: Carousel — the pilot's name on a client switch
Description: With the live picture off, switching to another EVE client blurs the power display's animation and shows that pilot's name for five seconds. Switch several times in a row and the blur stays, only the name changes.

[alerts]  alerts.webm / alerts-poster.jpg
Title: Sounds You Choose
Feature: Optional alert sounds and app volume
Description: Settings → General → Alerts: a sound for a faction spawn and one for a re-warp with no damage, both off by default with a play key to preview, and one Volume slider for every sound of the app. (The clip is silent.)
